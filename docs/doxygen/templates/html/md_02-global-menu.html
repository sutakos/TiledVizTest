Global menu

All TiledViz icons

The menu lies on the left side of the interface. For each option or item in the global menu, the tag menu or the tile menu, the icon will appear white if the option is not selected or used, and grey if it is selected. To select a menu item, you have to click on it. If you entered an option and do not wish anymore to go further with it, clicking on the grey icon will exit it.

Filter with keywords

Search icon

Type keywords in the research bar, they will be matched with a list of keywords extracted from the meta-data of the tiles. Auto-completion is enabled for this ﬁeld. The “&” can be used to combine several keywords. Upon hitting “Enter”, the tiles corresponding to the keywords are moved to the top, and little stickers appears on their right side to show to which keyword(s) they ﬁt. The legend for the colors is shown at the top right of the interface.

Filter node through tags and zoom on filtered nodes

  1. Launch a simple research

    type the keyword in the search bar and press enter :

    the matchiang nodes will move to the top of the grid, the other ones will stay at the bottom and become darker.

  2. Launch a multiple research

    type the keywords in the search bar, using the symbol "&" as separator ("pressure&heat" to search for both terms) :

    a legend with diﬀerent colors will be shown at the top of the screen.

    Clean search : click on the Eraser icon on the upper-right corner.

    This ﬁlter use Jquery UI modules : autocompletes, see https://jqueryui.com/autocomplete/ for details
